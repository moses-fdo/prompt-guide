## 1. What is a "Prompt" and How Does AI Think?

* **What is a prompt?**  
  A prompt is simply the instruction or message you type into the AI chat box (like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude).
* **How does AI work?**  
  Think of the AI like a **smart intern who knows many facts, but knows nothing about what you want until you tell them clearly**.
* If you give a lazy instruction like: `"Help me write my paper"`, the AI has to guess what you mean. Usually, it gives back a generic, boring answer.
* If you tell it **who it is**, **what to read**, and **how to format the answer**, it gives you amazing work in 5 seconds.

## 2. The Simple 4-Step Formula to Write a Good Prompt

Whenever you ask an AI for help, include these 4 things:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 THE 4-PART PROMPT FORMULA                   │
├───────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. WHO (Role) │ Tell the AI who it should pretend to be.    │
│ 2. WHAT (Task)│ Tell it exactly what job to do.             │
│ 3. RULES      │ Tell it what NOT to do (keep it simple).    │
│ 4. FORMAT     │ Tell it how the final answer should look.   │
└───────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### An Easy Everyday Example:
* **1. WHO:** "Act as a friendly high school science teacher."
* **2. WHAT:** "Explain how photosynthesis works."
* **3. RULES:** "Do not use complicated words. Use an analogy to baking a cake."
* **4. FORMAT:** "Give me 3 short bullet points."

## 3. Bad Prompt vs. Good Prompt (Real Examples)

### Example 1: Reading a Research Paper

❌ **Bad Prompt:**  
> *"Summarize this paper."*  
> *(Why it fails: The AI gives you a long, boring wall of text that is just as confusing as the original paper.)*

✅ **Good Prompt:**  
> *"Act as a friendly college professor. Read the text below from this research paper.*  
> *1. Tell me in 2 simple sentences what problem the authors are solving.*  
> *2. Tell me the main result they found.*  
> *3. Explain it like I am a 15-year-old student, without any confusing jargon.*  
> *Here is the text: [PASTE TEXT]"*

---

### Example 2: Making Slides

❌ **Bad Prompt:**  
> *"Make me a PPT about climate change."*  
> *(Why it fails: You get 20 slides filled with paragraphs nobody can read.)*

✅ **Good Prompt:**  
> *"I have to give a 5-minute presentation about solar energy to my classmates.*  
> *Give me an outline for 5 slides.*  
> *For each slide, give me:*  
> *- A short title (maximum 4 words)*  
> *- Only 2 or 3 short bullet points (under 8 words each)*  
> *- What I should say out loud in 30 seconds."*

## 4. Ready-to-Use Prompts (Just Copy and Paste!)

You can copy these prompts directly, paste your text into them, and hit Enter.

### Prompt 1: Explain a Hard Research Paper in Plain Words
> Use this when you download a paper and cannot understand the complex academic language.

```markdown
Act as a helpful tutor. I am a student trying to understand this research paper.

Here is the abstract and introduction of the paper:
---
[PASTE PAPER ABSTRACT HERE]
---

Please explain it to me by answering these 4 simple questions:
1. What real-world problem are the authors trying to solve?
2. What new method or idea did they try?
3. What was their main finding or result? (Give concrete numbers if mentioned).
4. What is one limitation or weakness of this study?

Rules: Use plain, everyday English. Do not use complicated technical jargon unless you immediately explain what it means in brackets.
```

---

### Prompt 2: Explain a Confusing Math Formula or Idea
> Use this when you see an equation, graph, or theory that makes no sense.

```markdown
You are a patient teacher. I do not understand this formula/concept from my research paper:

---
[PASTE THE FORMULA, CODE, OR EXPLANATION HERE]
---

Please help me understand it:
1. Explain the main idea in simple words using an everyday real-life story or analogy.
2. List what each letter or symbol means in plain English.
3. Give me a simple example using easy numbers (like 1, 2, 5) so I can see how it works step-by-step.
```

---

### Prompt 3: Compare Multiple Papers for Your Literature Review
> Use this when your teacher asks you to read 3 or 4 papers and write a report comparing them.

```markdown
I am writing a research report. Here are short summaries of 3 papers I found:

Paper 1: [PASTE SHORT SUMMARY OR ABSTRACT 1]
Paper 2: [PASTE SHORT SUMMARY OR ABSTRACT 2]
Paper 3: [PASTE SHORT SUMMARY OR ABSTRACT 3]

Please compare them for me:
1. Put them into a simple table with these columns:
   - Paper Name
   - What they tested
   - What they found
   - What their weakness was
2. In 2 simple paragraphs, tell me: Where do these papers agree, and what did all of them miss?
```

---

### Prompt 4: Turn a Paper into Slides for a Presentation
> Use this to quickly prepare a class presentation or seminar talk.

```markdown
I have to give a 5-minute presentation about this paper to my class.

Here is what the paper is about:
---
[PASTE PAPER SUMMARY OR KEY POINTS]
---

Create a 6-slide presentation outline for me:
For each slide provide:
- Slide Title: (Max 4 words)
- On-Slide Text: (Only 2 or 3 short bullet points, very easy to read)
- Picture Idea: (What picture or chart should I put on this slide?)
- What to Say: (Write 2 or 3 sentences of what I should say out loud to the audience)
```

## 5. How to Stop AI from "Lying" (Making Things Up)

Sometimes AI makes things up. In AI language, this is called a **"Hallucination"**.  
*For example, if you ask it for 5 papers on a topic, it might invent fake titles, fake authors, and fake years that do not exist!*

### How to protect yourself:
Whenever you ask AI to help with your school work or research, **always add this short rule at the end of your message**:

```markdown
IMPORTANT RULE: 
Only use facts from the text I gave you. 
If the answer is not in the text, do not guess or make anything up. Just say: "This is not mentioned in the text."
```

### Detailed Breakdown by Use Case:

#### 1. Reading Huge Books, Theses & Long Papers (Context Window)
* **What you need:** An AI that can hold hundreds of pages without forgetting the beginning.
* 🟢 **Best Free Choice:** **Google Gemini 2.0 / 1.5 Flash** (via [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)) or **Google NotebookLM**. Gemini has a 1-million word memory. You can upload an entire 300-page book or thesis in one prompt for $0.
* 💎 **Best Paid Choice ($20/mo):** **Gemini Advanced / Gemini 1.5 Pro** or **Claude Pro**. Slightly better at very subtle nuances in massive documents.

---

#### 2. Writing Clean Academic Reports & Essays (Tone & Style)
* **What you need:** An AI that sounds like an educated human, not a robotic machine that uses cheesy words like *"delve"*, *"tapestry"*, or *"crucial testament"*.
* 🟢 **Best Free Choice:** **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** (via [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) free tier). Hands down the most natural, human-like writer.
* 💎 **Best Paid Choice ($20/mo):** **Claude 3.7 Sonnet** (Anthropic Claude Pro). Unmatched writing quality and structure for research papers and dissertations.

---

#### 3. Solving Hard Math Formulas, Proofs & Data Code
* **What you need:** A "reasoning model" that thinks step-by-step before answering.
* 🟢 **Best Free Choice:** **DeepSeek-R1** (via [chat.deepseek.com](https://chat.deepseek.com)). 100% free with no subscription required. It shows its full thinking process and outperforms almost every other free model on hard math and coding problems.
* 💎 **Best Paid Choice ($20/mo):** **OpenAI o1 / o3-mini** (ChatGPT Plus). The world benchmark for competitive math, logic puzzles, and complex algorithm verification.

---

#### 4. Finding Real Facts, Live Sources & Web Links
* **What you need:** An AI connected to the live internet that puts a real link on every claim so you can verify it.
* 🟢 **Best Free Choice:** **Perplexity AI** (via [perplexity.ai](https://perplexity.ai)) or **Microsoft Copilot**. Type any question, and it gives you a clean summary with clickable numbers pointing to real websites and news articles.
* 💎 **Best Paid Choice ($20/mo):** **Perplexity Pro ($20/mo)**. Lets you switch between top models (Claude 3.7, GPT-4o, Sonar) with deeper search depth.

### How to Add and Use the Canva Plugin / Canva GPT

If you want to tell AI to make your slides and automatically open them inside Canva, you can use the **Canva Plugin / GPT**. Here is how to do it step-by-step:

#### Option A: Inside ChatGPT (Using the Canva GPT)
1. Open ChatGPT (web or app).
2. Click on **"Explore GPTs"** in the left sidebar.
3. In the search bar at the top, type: `Canva`.
4. Click on the official **Canva** GPT (made by `canva.com`).
5. Click **"Start Chat"**.
6. Type a simple prompt like:
   > *"Make a 5-slide modern presentation for university students about Renewable Energy. Keep it clean with a blue theme."*
7. The AI will think for 10 seconds and give you 2 to 4 clickable design cards.
8. **Click on any design:** It immediately opens Canva in your browser with all the slides already created! You can edit every title, change colors, or download it as a PowerPoint file.

#### Option B: Inside Canva Directly (Canva Magic Design - 100% Free)
If you don't have ChatGPT Plus, you can do this directly on Canva for free:
1. Go to [canva.com](https://canva.com) and log in.
2. Click **"Create a design"** $\rightarrow$ choose **"Presentation (16:9)"**.
3. In the left panel, click on **"Design"**, then look for **"Magic Design"** (it has a sparkle icon ✨).
4. Type what your presentation is about: *(e.g., "5 slides summarizing my research on artificial intelligence in healthcare")*.
5. Canva will generate a full set of editable slides with photos, fonts, and bullet points ready to go!

### How to Make a PPT Truly Editable Using an AI Agent

When people use AI to generate presentations, they often get stuck because:
- The AI gives them text in chat, but they don't know how to turn it into slides.
- Or the AI gives an image or PDF where you cannot click and change the words.

Here are the **3 easiest ways to get a 100% editable `.pptx` PowerPoint file** using AI:

#### Method 1: Ask an AI Agent to use `python-pptx` (The Pro Developer Way)
If you are using an AI coding tool or agent (like Antigravity, Cursor, Claude Code, or ChatGPT Code Interpreter), you don't have to build slides by hand!

Just give the agent this exact prompt:
```markdown
Write and run a Python script using the "python-pptx" library to create an editable PowerPoint presentation named "my_presentation.pptx".

Requirements:
- 16:9 widescreen layout
- Clean dark background (dark navy blue #0F172A) with white readable text
- Slide 1: Title slide ("AI in Modern Medicine")
- Slide 2: The Core Problem (3 bullet points with large font)
- Slide 3: Results & Data (Include a clean 3x3 table with real numbers)
- Slide 4: Conclusion & Next Steps
Make sure all text boxes and table cells are real native PowerPoint shapes so I can open the file in Microsoft PowerPoint and edit them.
```
*What happens:* The AI agent writes and runs the code. It creates a real `.pptx` file on your computer. You double-click it, and it opens right inside PowerPoint or Google Slides where every single word is editable!

---

#### Method 2: The Secret "PowerPoint VBA Macro" Trick (Works in standard ChatGPT / Claude!)
You don't need any coding tools installed for this trick. It works directly inside Microsoft PowerPoint:

1. Go to ChatGPT or Claude and type:
   > *"Write a PowerPoint VBA Macro code to create a 5-slide presentation on [YOUR TOPIC]. Include clean titles and 3 short bullet points per slide."*
2. Copy the code the AI gives you.
3. Open Microsoft PowerPoint on your laptop.
4. Press `Alt + F11` on your keyboard (or `Fn + Option + F11` on Mac). This opens a small window.
5. In the top menu, click **Insert $\rightarrow$ Module**.
6. Paste the code into the big empty box.
7. Press `F5` on your keyboard (or click the green "Play" ▶ button).
8. Close the window and look at PowerPoint: **All your slides are instantly created with fully editable text boxes!**

---

#### Method 3: Export from Gamma or Canva to `.pptx`
If you made slides in **Gamma** or **Canva**:
- In **Gamma**: Click the **Share** button (top right) $\rightarrow$ **Export** $\rightarrow$ **Export to PowerPoint (.pptx)**.
- In **Canva**: Click **Share** (top right) $\rightarrow$ **More** $\rightarrow$ scroll down and click **Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx)** $\rightarrow$ **Download**.
- Both files open in PowerPoint with editable text boxes, not flat frozen pictures!

## 10. Step-by-Step: From an Idea to a Final Presentation for $0

Here is the exact step-by-step path you can follow for any school or university project:

```
Step 1: Find Papers (15 mins)
   👉 Go to Semantic Scholar or ResearchRabbit.
   👉 Find 3 to 5 good papers on your topic and download the PDFs.

Step 2: Save Citations (2 mins)
   👉 Save them into Zotero so you have all the author names and dates ready.

Step 3: Study the Papers with AI (20 mins)
   👉 Open Google NotebookLM (free).
   👉 Upload the PDFs.
   👉 Use "Prompt 1: Explain in Plain Words" and "Prompt 3: Compare Papers".
   👉 NotebookLM will give you a clear summary where every sentence links to the exact page.

Step 4: Write Your Report (1 hour)
   👉 Open Google Docs.
   👉 Organize your sections: Introduction, What Others Did, Our Method, Results, Conclusion.
   👉 Add your citations using Zotero.

Step 5: Make Your Slides (15 mins)
   👉 Copy your main takeaways into Gamma.app or use "Prompt 4: Turn a Paper into Slides".
   👉 Keep text short (maximum 3 bullet points per slide!).
   👉 Practice speaking your talk out loud.
```

## 💡 Quick Tips for Beginners

1. **Be Specific:** Never say `"write about cars"`. Say `"write 3 bullet points explaining why electric cars save money on fuel compared to petrol cars"`.
2. **One Job at a Time:** Don't ask the AI to write your whole 10-page report in one prompt. Ask for an outline first, then do one section at a time.
3. **Always Check the Facts:** AI is your helpful assistant, not your replacement. Always double-check names, numbers, and dates in your original paper.
4. **Never Pay When Free Exists:** Google NotebookLM, Zotero, Semantic Scholar, and PDFgear are 100% free and better than most paid apps.
